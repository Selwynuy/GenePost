Refund policy

How to request a refund when GenePost purchases are available.

Prepared November 9, 2026

Request within 14 days

If GenePost is not a fit, email support@genetrify.com within 14 calendar days of purchase. Include the order number and the email used for checkout. Keep your full license key and card details private.

How refunds are handled

We verify the order and process eligible refunds through the checkout provider to the original payment method. Your bank or payment provider determines when the refund appears. A refunded license is disabled. Your local drafts and media stay on your Mac.

Consumer rights

This policy preserves any rights or remedies under applicable consumer law, including for defective or misdescribed software. Contact support if you need help with installation, activation, or an order.
