Privacy policy

How GenePost handles your workspace, AI requests, and license.

Prepared November 9, 2026

Who operates GenePost

GenePost is operated by GENETRIFY INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY SERVICES, Polomolok, South Cotabato, Philippines. For privacy questions or requests, email support@genetrify.com.

Your Mac workspace

GenePost stores your drafts, captions, media, channel identities and URLs, scheduling history, agent conversations, and preferences in a local workspace. The license key and activation record are kept in your Mac’s Keychain. You control local backups and can remove local workspace data through Finder after quitting GenePost. Keep a backup if you want to retain your work.

AI and social platforms

When you run an AI task, GenePost sends the input needed for that task to Codex using your own account. This may include prompts, relevant writing notes, previous captions, media, and selected channel information. The connected provider processes those inputs under its own terms and privacy policy. The local MCP integration lets an authorized Codex or Claude Code session read or change workspace content using the tools you allow.

Publishing opens the channel’s website in your browser. The social platform handles sign-in, publication, and public visibility under its own policies. Your browser’s login credentials stay with your browser and the platform.

Purchases and activation

When checkout is available, Lemon Squeezy processes the purchase as merchant of record. It collects the contact, payment, tax, and order details needed for checkout. We can access order information needed to deliver your license, support it, and meet recordkeeping obligations. GenePost does not receive full payment card details.

Activation and verification send the license key and a randomly named activation or instance identifier to Lemon Squeezy. We use this to verify your license and manage its device limit. The licensing request does not include your drafts, Mac serial number, or social account credentials.

Website and support

The browser demo keeps sample edits and media in the current tab. It makes no AI or publishing requests and does not upload files you select.

This website serves static pages and uses no advertising or analytics cookies. The hosting provider may process IP addresses, request times, and browser details for delivery and security. When you email us, we use your address and message to respond and resolve your request. We retain purchase and support records as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. Providers may process data in other countries under their own policies.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access or correct personal data we hold, object to processing, or request deletion or restriction where applicable. We may verify your identity and keep records required by law. Contact us at the privacy address above. You may also contact the National Privacy Commission.

Updates

We’ll post revisions here when our practices change. The date above identifies this version.
